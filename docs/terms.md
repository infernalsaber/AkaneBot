# Akane Bot Terms of Service

_Effective: 23 November 2026 · Last updated: 23 November 2026_

Hi! These are the ground rules for using Akane Bot ("Akane", "the bot", "we"). We've kept them short and in plain words.

By adding Akane to a server or using any of its commands, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, that's okay, just don't use the bot (or remove it from your server).

**The short version:** be nice, don't abuse the bot, follow Discord's rules, and understand that Akane is a free hobby project run by one person, provided as it is.

---

## 1. Who can use Akane

You need to be old enough to use Discord under [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms). By using Akane, you also agree to follow Discord's [Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines).

---

## 2. Using Akane fairly

Please don't:

- spam commands, or try to overload, crash or slow down the bot
- look for and exploit bugs or security holes (if you find one, please tell us instead, we'll be grateful!)
- use Akane to harass, threaten or target other people
- use Akane to post anything illegal, or anything that breaks Discord's rules
- add emotes or images to servers that you don't have the right to use
- automate Akane with scripts or self-bots, or try to get around its limits

---

## 3. For server admins

When you add Akane to a server, you're responsible for how it's used there. That includes which permissions you give it and which channels it can see. Akane only does what its commands and permissions allow, so keep the permissions it asks for and you'll be fine.

---

## 4. Content Akane shows you

Most of what Akane shows comes from other services, such as AniList, MyAnimeList, VNDB, SauceNAO, trace.moe, MangaPlus and Miyoushe.

- **We don't own that content.** It belongs to those services and to the people who made it.
- **We can't promise it's accurate or complete.** If a score, date or source looks wrong, it's most likely wrong at the source.
- **Some results may be meant for adults.** Akane keeps those to age-restricted channels.

When Akane fixes the embed of a link someone posts, it only shows a preview (title, description, cover image) and always links back to the original. If you own content and would rather Akane didn't preview it, contact us and we'll sort it out.

Akane is free and run in our spare time. We do our best to keep it running, but:

- features may change, or be removed
- other services Akane relies on may go down or change, and that can break things

Akane is provided **"as is"**, without any guarantees.
